Sermon transcript

Full transcript of the message preached by Carlos Barrera.

Introduction

Peace of Christ, peace of Christ. How is everyone? Well, we're not here at a funeral. This is where life breathes. I'm going to ask you to glorify God in this moment, because we need this atmosphere to change, because here there is freedom. The Bible says that where the Spirit is, there is freedom. I believe that today we will see powerful things in this place. Glory to His name.

Listen, today I'm going to preach to you a somewhat delicate topic, but I know that God will give me the grace to preach it. It has not been easy, because when one gets into the spiritual realm it is a bit more difficult, but I know that God is with us. The Spirit of God is here in this place. I'm going to ask for your prayers while I'm preaching, so that it may be the Holy Spirit speaking to our lives.

Today I'm going to preach to you on the topic: "I Need A Change Now."

How many of us, at some point in our lives, have reached a moment of desperation where we said "I need a change now"? That means there is an emergency. I want you to pay attention to every word that God is going to speak to your life, because it is God who is speaking, not the man here. It is God who is going to speak through my life with this word.

I believe that for all of us who preach, God speaks to us in different ways. The Lord has also spoken to me in different ways. He has given me words back there, when someone is preaching; I have told Luis: "You know, Luis, God gave me a word, I don't know when I'm going to use it, but the Lord gave it to me." But this day was different.

I left work, outside, when there was all the movement about immigration coming, and people started to get scared. Even Christians started to get scared, knowing that there was a God who rescued us from the most vile and despised. Can't He save us if He has the last word? Then I said: "I'm going to go, Lord." And yes, I'm going to be sincere, there was some fear, because I have my family, they depend on me. But I was trusting in God that God was going to be with me. I went on the plane and everything normal, I passed security and everything normal, I got to work at the hotel.

And the second day I was there, at 3 in the morning, I had a dream where I heard someone cry out: "I need a change now!" But it was a cry of desperation. At that moment I got up and started writing what had come to my mind, because sometimes we forget. Suddenly came the story of the Gadarene demoniac. There was a cry in that man. We're going to see that more ahead in the preaching.

But before I start, I would like you to join me in prayer. Stand up, if you wish, please.

Opening Prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, every word that comes out of Your mouth, Lord, You send it with a purpose. Eternal Father, Your Word says that it does not return empty, but comes to fulfill a purpose when You send it. I thank You, Lord, for trusting in this person who is here speaking, with my defects and my sins, and yet You still see something in me. I ask You, Lord, that You be You, through Your Holy Spirit, flowing through my lips. That the word You have deposited in my heart today brings freedom, brings change. That You bring restoration to someone, Eternal Father, in the powerful name of Jesus Christ. Amen. Glory to God, be seated.

God Knows You

You know that many times, brothers, all of us who before knowing God, hiding ourselves, drinking I don't know how many beers to show our friends that we are the best, but inside we are tearing ourselves apart.

So, so that you know that God is the one who knows you more than you imagine, I want us to read this word in the book of Psalms 139, and I want you to analyze every word, because it is the Word of God. Psalm 139, from 13 to 16. But continue glorifying God. If you're already there, tell me amen. Listen to what the Word of God says:

"For You formed my inmost being; You knitted me in my mother's womb. I praise You, so wonderfully You made me; wonderful are Your works! My very self You knew; my bones were not hidden from You, When I was being made in secret, fashioned in the depths of the earth. Your eyes foresaw all my days; before one came into being, You planned them all."

Psalm 139:13-16

Let me tell you that your innermost being knows God. What God created, He knows, and He knows who is his Creator. So if the world has made you believe that you are no longer good for anything, as long as your Creator does not say otherwise, because He is your Creator, He is the one who has the last word. "Your eyes foresaw all my days; before one came into being, You planned them all." Does anyone here lack an eye, right? Although we seem to have the same things, God made each of us authentic and special. You are not the same as someone else, even if you are twins in appearance; there is something different. You are special.

The Gadarene Demoniac

This is what I wanted so that we would understand what God wants to speak to our lives today. I want to speak to you through the story of the Gadarene demoniac. I've been reading, because it's in three gospels: Mark, Matthew and Luke. Each one sees it differently, but I said: I'm going to read why this happens. Many times we only stay with what's on the surface, and sometimes I wondered: how did this man come to this state? Would he be a sorcerer, or someone? We don't know. But right now, through the preaching, perhaps someone is going to identify with the state in which one day he was in his life, or is at this moment. But God today has the power to give him freedom.

The Bible says that Jesus was on the other side and told his apostles that they were going to go to the other side. But if you notice, when Jesus headed to the place of the Gadarenes there was a storm, there was opposition. But they didn't know that the one who was in the boat had the power to bring down everything, because He has power over all things. He has authority even over darkness, He has authority over Satan himself. He does not have authority over those who have believed in Christ Jesus. He has no authority over your life, unless you give it to him.

The Bible says that this man lived in the tombs, lived in the place of the pigs, because the Bible speaks of pigs on the side of where he was. Can you imagine this man, how did he come to that state? I believe that deep inside, in his moment of lucidity, he said: "I need someone to help me." Do you know how people helped him? They put chains on him, tied him with shackles and chains, he says, but he broke them, because the spirit he had in him was so strong that he already had dominion over him.

And many of us who are here one day had a spirit that already dominated our own lives, even though we had family, and we didn't care; we went over all those things, because that demon, or that spirit that was tormenting his life, was more powerful than his own will. Because the unclean spirit is going to take away your authority, it's going to take away your worth, but God is going to give you something better: He's going to give you a worth that the world cannot give you.

The Bible says that he went day and night cutting himself, hurting himself. But I feel that there is someone here in this place who came today like that Gadarene demoniac, asking for help, asking to say: "I need a change in my life now." Perhaps you have not been able to express it, but in your heart, and the God who knows you is telling you today: today I want to reach you.

The Bible says that when Jesus arrived at the place of the Gadarenes, it was not the man who came to Him; it was the demon that was in the Gadarene. It was the demons who came toward Jesus running and saying to Him: "Jesus, Son of the Most High God, what do you have to do with me?" That tells me that the demon knew who Jesus was. The demon has no doubt about who Jesus is. The demon has no uncertainty about Jesus. But there are people who call themselves believers who have more doubt than faith. Glory to God!

And the Bible says that Jesus asked him: "What is your name?" Because his name was Legion. Tell the demon: "What is your name?" Because his name was Legion. A legion is 6 thousand demons. Imagine this: 6 thousand demons tormenting the life of one person. And the Bible says that the demon begged Jesus not to send them out of the region. And Jesus, who had all the power, allowed those 6 thousand demons to enter the pigs. And immediately the pigs rushed into the sea and drowned. That tells you that God's power is so great that even in a pig, that power can have effect.

And when the people came to that place, they found the man who was clothed, in his right mind, sitting at the feet of Jesus, glorifying God. That is the change that God wants to make in your life today! From desperation to restoration. From torment to peace. From darkness to light. From death to life. That is the change God has for you.

The Healing Is Complete

And do you know what was surprising in this story? That when Jesus was about to leave, that man asked Him to let him go with Him. But Jesus told him: "Go home to your own people and tell them how much the Lord in his mercy has done for you." And that man went throughout the region proclaiming how much Jesus had done for him. And the people were amazed.

So, brother, sister, if God has freed you, if God has given you a change, it's not for you to keep it to yourself. It's for you to go out and proclaim to others how great our God is. Because there are many people who need to hear that there is hope. There are many people who need to know that God has the power to transform a life.

I want to end by saying that the change God is going to give you is not a temporary change. It's not a change that will last a few days, a week, or a month. It's a change that will be eternal. It's a change of eternal life. It's a change where your spirit, your soul and your body are going to be healthy. Because God wants to bless all of you. God doesn't want you to be well in one part and bad in another. God wants you to be completely free.

If there is someone here today who came saying "I need a change now", I want you to know that this change is in Jesus. Jesus is the one who has the power. Jesus is the one who has the authority. Jesus is the one who knows you. And Jesus is the one who loves you. So if today you want to give your life to Jesus, if today you want to receive that change, raise your hand, because God is looking for you. Glory to His name!

Closing

Let us raise our hands toward heaven and let us tell God yes. That yes we want that change. That yes we want that freedom. That yes we want that new life that only Jesus can give. Because God has a purpose for your life. God loves you. And God wants you to be free. In the name of Jesus, amen.
