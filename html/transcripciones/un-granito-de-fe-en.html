Sermon transcript

Full transcript of the message preached by Omar Robles.

Transcript of the sermon. Bible verses are in quotation marks.

Introduction

How are you all doing today? Glory to God. Hallelujah. Why don’t we stand up, brothers and sisters? We’re going to pray. But before we do, greet the person next to you. Tell them to buy you a coffee, because the weather calls for it right now—and make sure that coffee comes with a delicious little pastry. Glory to God. You liked the idea of the pastry, didn’t you? Hallelujah. Now let’s pray.

Thank you, Lord Jesus, King and Almighty God, for your love, Lord, and for this moment you have given us. Thank you for allowing us to be in your presence. Blessed are you, mighty King. Lord, I ask that the seed of faith we are going to share today would fall on fertile ground. May it fall into those hearts, grow, and bear fruit a thousandfold. I ask this in the mighty name of Jesus Christ, Lord. To you be the glory and honor. Amen. You may sit down, brothers and sisters.

Today we’re going to talk about faith. I’ve titled this sermon “A Little Mustard Seed of Faith.”

Many of us probably already know what faith is, because faith is what brought you here today, to this gathering, to this church. But I want you to take this seed of faith with you. Even though it’s very small—faith is very small—some of you may already have greater faith, the size of a tree. We’ll talk about all of that in a moment. But when we’re just starting out, our faith is very small and hardly noticeable. We might even be walking down the street, see someone who’s having problems, and usually tell them, “Have faith.” We say it out of habit, but do we really know what faith is? Sometimes we say, “Yes, I know what faith is: it’s believing in God.” But do we truly believe it, or can we only say the words?

So today we’re going to talk about what faith can do. We need only faith as small as a mustard seed to do wonderful things, to see miracles—and to see those miracles in our homes, with our families, and among everyone around us. We’ll begin to see how powerful faith can be, even when it’s so small.

The Seed We’re Giving Away

I brought a seed today. Let me see if I didn’t lose it. Come on, bro. Can you see this seed? No. You can’t see it with the naked eye, but only the person who has it can see how great their faith is. And today I want you to take this seed of faith with you. I’m not going to give it to you literally, okay? The point is to take it with you spiritually and plant it in your heart. Glory to God.

Look, there are lots of things for sale out there, lots of advertising. You can find motivational speeches and talks everywhere, but you have to pay for them, because nothing is free—especially in this country. Maybe back in our hometown, some things are free, but in this country nothing is free. And those motivational talks may work for a while, but they’re sold to you. Listen, I’m not going to sell you anything today. I’m giving you this wonderful word that the Lord gave me, and he gave it to me in a life group, which is the best thing.

How many of us like free things? Well, today you’re going to take home free seeds. And you know what’s best? You can give that seed to anyone you want, and it’s free. All we need to do is take care of it. We’ll take it with us, plant it in our hearts, and help it grow in our minds—but we have to water it. What will we water it with? A few minutes spent reading the Word. That’s free. And to help it grow stronger, I encourage you to join a life group. Why? Because if you water the seed of faith with a few minutes in the Bible, and also attend a life group, your faith will grow stronger. For faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word.

My Testimony

Look, I received this word eight years ago. Eight years ago, I planted that word in my heart, and I received it in a life group. I came to that life group feeling uneasy. I thought they just wanted my money because, as I was saying, nothing is free in this country. Then I arrived at the life group, and they welcomed me with coffee and pastries, and sometimes even a meal. I thought, “How much is this whole buffet going to cost me?” So I arrived as a skeptic, practically without faith.

But when they began reading this verse to me, which I’m going to share with you now—look, let’s read it. Matthew 17:20. Write it down on your phone so you can remember it later, okay? Matthew 17:20 says:

“He replied, ‘Because you have so little faith. Truly I tell you, if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, “Move from here to there,” and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.’”

In this chapter, the Lord Jesus Christ is correcting his disciples because they couldn’t drive out a spirit that was tormenting this young man. But when I heard this verse at the life group, I realized that I could have been that young man described in the Bible, because I had very little faith; I was a skeptic. And I realized that the spirit tormenting that young man was like a spirit tormenting me, too. How? It wasn’t as though I was climbing the walls or anything like that. But it affected my emotions, my mood, and my decisions. What kind of decisions?

Look, I would often spend time with friends who invited me to drink and smoke, and I couldn’t say no. It was hard for me to say no. I would think, “How long am I going to keep falling into these same habits? When am I going to put a stop to all this that harms me and my family?” Because when they said, “Just have one,” they were very convincing. They would tell me, “One won’t hurt you.” But the problem was that once I had one, I couldn’t stop. This spirit really shook me and tormented me. I’d get home, and it was the same problems. And this kept happening, every weekend, as it happened. Those of you who laughed know what I’m talking about, right?

Then, through that study, I understood that if I had faith as small as a mustard seed, I could put a stop to all that. I could put a stop to the scarcity in my home, because I did work. But I would think, “Why am I always short on money? Why is my household struggling financially?” Well, I understood that the spirit that came and shook me also shook my wallet, because my friends would come around, invite me to dances, and I would obediently go. Once I got there, what got shaken was half my paycheck. So I began to realize that it was important to plant that faith in my heart and let it grow in my mind, so I could put a stop to things that way.

I left that house and claimed that verse as my mustard seed of faith. I could say to any problem, “Move out of my way. Move.” And it has to move through the power of the Spirit of the Lord Jesus Christ. That was the beginning. And the more involved I became in the life group, the more I immersed myself in reading the Word, you know what? The stronger I became. And let me tell you, I was inconsistent, too. What does inconsistent mean? Sometimes I went to the life group, sometimes I didn’t; sometimes I went to church, sometimes I didn’t. But I realized that when I stayed committed to the church and to the life groups, I had more strength, more willpower, and more self-control. When I drifted away, it was easy to fall back into everything. That’s when I began making the right decisions. I said, “No, my faith has to grow.” It began as a tiny mustard seed, but as I watered it and nourished it, it grew stronger. And it wasn’t only my faith, but also the faith of my family, my children, and the people around me. Glory to you, Lord Jesus.

I came to the conclusion that I belonged to this congregation, and that my life belonged to Christ. When you put all of that together, it is powerful—you begin to see miracles. How many of you want to see miracles? We only need a very tiny seed, and we will see those miracles.

It’s not easy, but it’s not impossible

Now you may say, “Brother, you make it sound like everything is really easy.” No, it’s not easy. It’s not easy, but it’s not impossible. When you start with that faith, it grows as time goes by. Look, you’ll encounter stumbling blocks. There will be times when you even feel alone. Look, there will be times when your family lets you down, your brothers and sisters in the church let you down, and your life-group leader lets you down. Okay. But why? Because we’re human. We’re all going to let each other down, but the one who will never let you down is our Lord Jesus Christ. So you must put your faith in Him.

Look at what Luke 17 tells us. This is what Jesus said to His disciples:

“It is impossible but that offences will come: but woe unto him, through whom they come! It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend one of these little ones. Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him. And if he trespass against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him.”

And you know what the disciples said? The same thing I said:

“Lord, Increase our faith.”

Increase my faith, because in my human weakness, I really can’t do it. How can I forgive someone who keeps hurting me? How can I forgive someone who rejects me? How can I forgive someone who keeps insulting me? How can I do it? The disciples said, “Lord, how difficult is this? How can I do it?” And they said, “Lord, increase our faith.” And the Lord tells them:

“If ye had faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye might say unto this sycamine tree, Be thou plucked up by the root, and be thou planted in the sea; and it should obey you.”

What does that mean? There’s no problem. When you have faith, and your faith is growing, it doesn’t matter who insults you or hurts you: your flesh will feel it, but in your spirit you’ll be able to say—and the words will come from your lips—“You know what? I have nothing against you. I can forgive you. And if you sin against me seven times, I’ll forgive you seven times.” Church, it’s not easy, friends. It’s not easy, but it’s not impossible. Keep watering your faith; keep feeding it. That faith will grow.

At first it’s difficult, and everything in this life is difficult. Going to the gym feels difficult. How many of us have a gym membership? I have one. I’ve had it for about two years. I’ve gone maybe twice. I want results, but if I don’t work hard, if I don’t show up, how am I going to see those results? Faith is the same. If I don’t feed my faith, how will it grow? How will it grow to the point where I can forgive people when they offend me? It’s difficult, but let’s start little by little.

I decided to go to the gym and said, “I just need to go.” So I said, “I’m going to go, walk in, and leave. The next day I’ll go in, look around, and leave. I’ll do that for a week, and then the following week it’ll be a habit, and I’ll start little by little.” That’s how you begin with faith, too. You’ve come to church—glory to God. Make it a habit. Get used to this.

I’ll make it easier for you to keep feeding your faith. When you come to church, stop by for some coffee and bread. It’ll do you good, because you’ll feed your stomach, but don’t stop there. Come and feed your spirit, too. And we’re going to do that regularly. After you’ve been doing that for a month, if you’re not in a life group, join one. Once you’ve attended a life group for six months, you’ll see the results. But start with faith like this. Take that seed with you today and plant it in your heart, okay? Let this become part of you. You came today. Now start being consistent. Start going to your life group. In six months to a year, you’ll see a change in your life, your family, and the people around you.

From a tiny seed to a tree

Look, once we become consistent, our faith will grow so much that that little seed of faith will become a tree. Look, they’re going to put an image up for us. How big can your faith grow? Look at this tree. This is a mature tree. It started out so small you could hardly see it, but it can become a tree that withstands storms, hail, tempests, insults—anything the enemy wants to put into our minds. And you know what’s best about this? Once your faith grows as big as this tree, it will be able to give shade to everyone around you. It will be able to bear fruit—abundant fruit. It will be able to share faith with all your friends and everyone you know, because you’ll be so strong that even if someone in church offends you—even if it’s your brother, a family member, or anyone else—they won’t be able to drive you away from the church anymore. You’ll have deep roots. You’ll be able to do things you can’t even imagine right now, but you need to begin by planting that faith, that mustard seed.

What does the Lord ask of us to get started with something so small? But this is just the beginning, church, for those of us who have been in the faith for years. Let’s see what Mark 4:30–32 says:

“And he said, Whereunto shall we liken the kingdom of God? or with what comparison shall we compare it? It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, is less than all the seeds that be in the earth: But when it is sown, it groweth up, and becometh greater than all herbs, and shooteth out great branches; so that the fowls of the air may lodge under the shadow of it.”

Church, you who have been coming for one year, two years, three years: if you started out like this in the first year, and you’re still like this in the second year, and still like this in the third year, and if the fourth year has come and your faith is still like this, let me tell you that it’s time for your seed of faith to start growing, for your seed of faith to increase. The Lord asks us to start out like this, but He wants us to end up like that full-grown, strong tree. He doesn’t want us to stay like this. God’s children go from glory to glory, from one stage of growth to another, and we are designed to bear fruit—abundant fruit.

Minister Jorge preached to us last week that we need to remain connected to the vine, to Christ, in order to bear fruit, and that the fruit can be cut off if it isn’t cared for. The plant can dry up if it isn’t cared for. Today, don’t let the enemy dry up that seed of faith you’re going to plant today. Glory to You, Lord Jesus Christ.

God bless all the leaders who have risen up in this church and in other churches, because it was one of those leaders who planted that seed of faith in my heart, and that’s why I can preach this message to you today. And that can happen only in a life group, where your faith will be cared for and nurtured for an entire year. But after that year, your faith must be firmly rooted enough to withstand any storm, so the enemy won’t make you run away. You’ve run away a lot; you’ve fled many times. We haven’t faced our problems. I ran away for a long time. Many times the Lord called me; many times the Lord called me to serve, and I said to Him, “No, Lord, it’s not my time. I know that someday, but the moment hasn’t come yet.”

Don’t let that happen to you, dear friend who is here today. It took me ten years to understand this word and commit myself to the Lord. I regret not doing it sooner, because if we can now give shade to those who need it and offer them that seed of faith, I let ten years go by. How many people could I have given that little piece of faith, that seed of faith? Today, commit to carrying that seed of faith in your heart. Let this be your beginning. Start today with a prayer.

Altar Call

And if you lack faith, if you’ve been in the church for a while and feel you still need more faith, that’s not bad. It’s not bad to be honest. Let them pray for you. Come to this altar and let them pray for you. The disciples said, “Lord, increase our faith!” because the road is long and difficult. They didn’t feel capable. They were human, just like us. They didn’t feel capable of sharing a word of blessing. They were afraid. What would people say about them? But the Lord increased their faith so much that those twelve disciples were able to give their lives for the Lord Jesus Christ. Their faith was so great that it has endured and has been recorded in this book, which shows us that it can be done. For more than 2,000 years, this Word has endured. That means faith is powerful; it means that in Christ, it can be done. There is a different life in Christ; there is true change in Christ. If they could do it, if each of the leaders could do it, you can too. You can make a difference; you can make a difference in your home, with your family. But you have to start with that faith.

Let’s stand. May this Word fall on fertile soil, in a willing heart, ready to make a change, ready to make a difference. We’ve been told many times, “You can’t. You’re a drunk, and you’ll always be a drunk.” Your friends have planted labels on you. Your family may have labeled you in a certain way. But it’s time to show them and everyone around you. But before you show them, prove it to yourself today. Show yourself today that you can make a difference, that today you will begin, that today will be the beginning, that today you will plant that seed of faith, and that in a few months you’ll see the results of the beautiful faith you plant today.

I’m not just speaking words; I’m telling you through my actions. I am deeply blessed by the Lord’s grace. What my faith brought about that day in that life group was that my family could be here with me today, and that I could be with them. If I hadn’t made that change, alcohol would have destroyed me, drugs would have destroyed me. But I committed myself to my family, and today I can see my daughter praising the Lord. Today I can see my son’s faith. Before I go up, he tells me, “Father, you can do it, because Christ is with you.” That’s what faith brings about. That’s what the seed of faith brings about.

Today I invite you to come to this altar. Today I invite you to make that difference, to begin the change you’ve longed for. Yes, it can be done. Let’s pray right where you are.

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, you know our hearts; you know everything, Father, because we cannot deceive you, Lord. You know how difficult it is for me to make a change, to leave everything behind—all my bad habits and addictions. But at this moment, Lord Jesus, plant that seed of faith in my heart. At this moment, Lord, increase the faith I need to keep walking in your way, to keep going, Lord, and not turn away from you. May my family not turn away from you, Lord; may my children not turn away from you. May this faith being planted today remain in our hearts and help us in our daily lives, helping us face every problem, every situation.

Thank you, Father, for this beautiful faith. Do not turn away from us, Lord. Show us the way; guide us along that path, Lord Jesus Christ. No matter how difficult it may be, help me persevere, Lord, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ.

Oh, Lord Jesus, at this moment, Father, may that seed, Lord, fall, Father, on good soil, Lord, in a willing heart, Father, ready to keep seeking more of you, Father, at this hour. Each one of us here in your presence declares, Lord, that our faith will grow. May your wonders, Lord, be revealed in our lives.

If there is anyone here, Lord, in this place, doubting your power, Father, at this hour we declare that your power will be made manifest in that heart this morning. If there is anyone here, Lord, in this place, Father, who does not feel worthy, Lord, who does not feel they belong, this morning we declare, Father, that this seed will begin to sprout within them, Father. May your wonders be seen, Lord, in the name of Jesus Christ, Father.

Let no one tell you that you can’t. Don’t let them tell you that your family cannot be restored. He is the only one, the only one who has power in your life. He is the only one with the authority to heal that wound, to heal illnesses, to bring people out of depression. Don’t tell me it can’t be done, because I am a living testimony to the power of God. When God’s power is made manifest in someone’s life, what seems impossible to you, God makes possible. Don’t let them tell you God can’t. Don’t let them tell you God can’t do it. If we are standing here, it’s because our faith began like that mustard seed. We began, and although there were difficulties, pain, and sacrifice, He was the one who made it possible. He is the one who made that tree.

Let no one tell you that you can’t, in the name of Jesus Christ. Let no one tell you that you can’t. Don’t let your mind tell you that you can’t; don’t let your weakness tell you that you can’t. Don’t let them tell you that you aren’t worthy of a full life. It doesn’t matter what the people beside you say. It doesn’t matter, it doesn’t matter.
