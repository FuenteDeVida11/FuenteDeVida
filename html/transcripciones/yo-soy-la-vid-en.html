Sermon transcript

Full transcript of the message preached by Jorge Macias.

Sermon transcript. Bible verses are enclosed in quotation marks.

Introduction

The peace of Christ, brothers and sisters. What a blessing it is to gather in this place to worship and glorify the one true God, our Lord Jesus Christ. Amen. Brother, friend, God is good, and His love as a Father for His children is immense. And His mercies, the Bible says, are new every morning. That means His mercies last forever—not just for a single day. First Chronicles 16:34 says:

“Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His mercy endures forever.”

Glory to God. And because the Lord is good, His mercies are everlasting, and He is powerful, nothing is impossible for Him. So He can turn your mourning into dancing, your sadness into joy. I want to ask: How many of you are thankful to the Lord Jesus Christ today because He turned your mourning into dancing and your sadness into joy, into hope—because now we have an everlasting hope from the Lord Jesus Christ? Amen.

The Seven “I Am” Statements

Look, in the Word of God we find that the Lord Jesus Christ makes seven statements about Himself, known as the seven “I am” statements of the Lord Jesus Christ. He says: I am the door. I am the way. I am the truth. I am the light of the world. I am the good shepherd. I am the resurrection and the life. And I am the true vine.

Do you know why the Lord says He is the true vine? He doesn’t just say, “I am the vine”; He adds, “I am the true vine,” because He knew that even in the days when He walked on this earth, there were other vines, but they were false. The only true Vine is the Lord Jesus Christ. That is why He said, “I am the true vine.” There may be many vines in the world, but the true one is the Lord Jesus Christ.

As you can see, the theme of today’s sermon is “I am the true vine.” I’ll be using this vine my wife made—which cost her several burns on her fingers—as an illustration. And I believe the Lord Jesus Christ will speak to us today. How many of you are convinced, and how many have come with a heart ready to hear the Word of God? Glory to God. Please turn with me to the book of John, chapter 15, verses 1 through 8.

John 15:1–8

The Word of God says:

“I am the true vine, and my Father is the gardener. Every branch in me that bears no fruit, He removes; and every branch that bears fruit, He prunes so that it may bear more fruit. You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you. Remain in me, and I in you. Just as the branch cannot bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, neither can you unless you remain in me. I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever remains in me, and I in him, bears much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing. Whoever does not remain in me is thrown away like a branch and withers; the branches are gathered, thrown into the fire, and burned. If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, ask for whatever you wish, and it will be done for you. My Father is glorified by this: that you bear much fruit and so prove to be my disciples.”

Amen. Let us pray. Father, we thank You, Lord Jesus Christ. Thank You for these moments, Lord; thank You for allowing us to be in this place. We ask that Your Holy Spirit would speak to us today, Lord, and that as I share this Word, You would work miracles and bring healing, restoring hearts and marriages, Lord. We know You can do it; You are powerful, my Lord Jesus Christ. Use my mind and my lips, but above all, my heart, so that words of eternal life may come from here—words that build up Your people. Thank You, Lord, because You are worthy of all worship; You are worthy of all glory. We thank You in the name of Jesus. Amen. Glory to God.

United to the True Vine

In these verses—well, as the sermon goes on, I’ll return to these same verses. Here the Lord Jesus Christ is telling His disciples that when a person lives united to the true Vine, that person must change. When a person lives united to the true Vine, that person must bear fruit. Because if we are not united to the true Vine, there will be no fruit; instead, we will fall away from the true Vine and wither, like those leaves over there. That is what the Word of God says.

Beginning in chapter 14 of the book of John, the Lord is teaching them to believe in Him and trust Him. He tells them that He is going to His Father’s house, where there are many rooms. He teaches them many other things in chapter 14, and at the end of the chapter, in the very last verse, He says: “I won’t say anything more to you, because the ruler of this world is coming, and he has nothing against us.” And at the end He tells them, “Get up, let’s leave this place.” I imagine that when He said, “Get up, let’s go,” He passed by a vineyard and kept explaining and teaching them. That is why He used the example of a vine.

So it is very important to be united to the true Vine in order to bear fruit. And the true Vine is the Lord Jesus Christ. Glory to God. He is our true Vine, and you and I are the branches. If we are united to the Vine and remain in it until the end, then we will be with the Lord Jesus Christ for all eternity. Amen. That is why it is so important to be united to the true Vine: the Word of God says that if we separate ourselves from the true Vine, we will be able to do nothing and will not be able to bear fruit, because we will be separated from the One who produces the fruit.

The fruits of the Holy Spirit come into our lives when we have a relationship with God, when we remain united to the Vine. We cannot produce these fruits ourselves. We can’t go to Walmart or another store and say, “Give me some fruit,” or “Give me this fruit, which is patience,” or “Give me this fruit, which is kindness.” We can’t ask them to give us a particular fruit, because the fruits come from the true Vine. He is the One who produces them; He is the One who gives them. The Lord Jesus Christ is our true Vine, and each day that we remain in the true Vine, each day that you and I remain united to the true Vine, the Lord will keep giving us fruit—more fruit each day—in keeping with God’s desire and will.

From the Broad Way to the Narrow Way

There was a time when we lived without God in our hearts. We were people who had no freedom, living under the enslavement of the ways of this world. We were slaves of the ruler of this world. There was wickedness in us, there were lies and deceit in us, because we were slaves of the ruler of this world. But when the Lord Jesus Christ set His eyes on you and me and brought us into His way, the true way, the narrow way, we then had to walk in righteousness, integrity, truth, and love, doing the will of our Lord Jesus Christ. Glory to God.

The Word of God says that if we remain in Him, we will not only bear fruit; if we remain in Him and His words remain in us, we can ask Him for whatever we want. John 15:7 says:

“If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, ask for whatever you wish, and it will be done for you.”

Glory to God, brothers and sisters. How many of you can give the Lord Jesus Christ a round of applause for this Word? We can ask Him for whatever we want, but we have to remain in the Vine in order to do that. Glory to God.

When we remain united to God, He will keep cleansing us day by day. God cleanses us day by day. We don’t have to be perfect to come near to God, and we don’t have to know the whole Bible to be united to the Vine. Day by day, God will keep cleansing us so that we can bear fruit—much fruit. And when you and I bear more fruit, and each day seek the presence of God, each day seek the presence of the Holy Spirit, each day seek the fruits of the Holy Spirit—when you and I do this, our heavenly Father will be glorified through you and me. We just read it in John 15:8:

“My Father is glorified by this: that you bear much fruit and so prove to be my disciples.”

There are many ways to glorify God, and many of us think it’s only by dancing, only by raising our hands, only by saying, “Glory to God, hallelujah.” But our Father is also glorified when we bear fruit. When we bear fruit, He is glorified.

A Decision That Is Yours and Mine

You have the power to decide whether you will live under the authority of the prince of this world, or walk under the authority of the King of this world—the King of kings and Lord of lords, the Lord Jesus Christ. The decision is yours and mine. Amen. But in the end, we will all receive our reward according to what we have decided. Whether we live apart from the Vine or remain joined to the Vine, in the end we will all receive our reward. Here on earth, the decision is yours and mine: whether or not we join ourselves to Him.

God’s will and purpose is for every human being to walk according to His will, not according to the will of this world or the will of the prince of this world, but according to the will of the King. The Lord wants us to remain joined to the Vine, because there are many blessings when we do. It’s not only about bearing fruit—there are so many blessings when we remain joined to the Vine. The Vine will teach us to make better decisions in our lives. It is the Vine who teaches us to make better decisions.

The Example of Judas

The Bible gives us examples of people who decided not to join themselves to the Vine, or who separated themselves from the Vine. As a result of that decision, of being separated from the One who produces all the fruit of the Holy Spirit, they did not have a very happy ending. I’ll read you one example. Turn with me to Matthew, chapter 26, verses 14–16. Let’s look at a man who made a very bad decision because he was separated from the Vine. It says:

“Then one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests and said, ‘What will you give me if I hand him over to you?’ They assigned him thirty pieces of silver. From then on, he looked for an opportunity to hand him over.”

Brothers and sisters, here we see a person who walked with Jesus, ate at the same table as Jesus, lived with Jesus, and spent almost three and a half years with the true Vine. He ate the same food as Jesus. The Lord even put a piece of bread into his mouth. He was a leader under the Lord Jesus Christ. And he made a very bad decision. Why? Judas walked with Jesus, ate with Jesus, slept with Jesus, saw the miracles of the Lord Jesus Christ, saw him multiply the fish and the loaves—but he made a bad decision because Judas was not joined to the true Vine. He was only around the true Vine; he was not joined to Him. That is why he made that bad decision.

And this tells me that to bear fruit—to bear the fruit of the Holy Spirit—it isn’t enough to come to church. It isn’t enough to be in a Life Group, to have a ministry here in the church, to be a leader, to speak in tongues, to wear a veil, or to wear a suit. The most important thing for bearing fruit is that we must be joined to the true Vine. Glory to God. And if we want to bear quality fruit, excellent fruit—the kind of fruit the Lord Jesus Christ asks of us—then we must be joined to Him day after day so that we can bear fruit and glorify our Father.

Going back to the story of Judas: there was greed in his heart, envy, a lack of repentance, wickedness, a lack of love, lies, and betrayal, because he was not holding on to the true Vine. He was not joined to the One who can replace all those fruits with the fruit of the Holy Spirit. Glory to His name. That is why it is so important, brother, friend, to give our hearts to God. That is the key: we must give our hearts to the true Vine. It is so important to remain in the Vine, so that day after day the Lord Jesus Christ can give us fruit and many more blessings.

This story the Bible tells us about a man named Judas teaches us that, separated from Him, we will make bad decisions. What He wants, brother, is for us to join ourselves to Him, so that things may go well for us here on earth and we may make the best decisions we can. If we want to do something, we first have to ask Him whether or not we should do it.

Close to the Vine, but Not in the Vine

As I read this story, the Lord told me that many people come near the Vine. Many people are around the Vine, but they stay there, around the Vine; they don’t join themselves to the true Vine. They like coming to church. They like being in a Life Group, especially when there’s food, and especially when they can stay afterward for a little gossip and a chat. They have a leadership role: “This is the best preacher here at Fuente de Vida. He preaches so beautifully; they take him to lots of churches.” But it’s not enough to be around the true Vine. The most important thing is to be joined to the true Vine.

Everything is important. It’s important to come to church, to be in a Life Group, to lead a ministry, to preach the Word of God, to speak in tongues, to dress like a Christian, to behave like a Christian, and to attend the women’s and men’s seminars. Everything is important, and we should do it all. But the most important thing is to be connected to the true Vine so that we can bear fruit—and much fruit. Glory to God.

And as I studied this teaching, the Lord gave me another word. I don’t know if the same thing happens nowadays; I don’t know. In the days of the Lord Jesus Christ, when He walked here on earth, the Word of God says that crowds of people followed Him. So many people followed Him. But many in that crowd followed Him because they were looking for a miracle. Many followed Him because they needed healing. Many followed Him because He gave them food; many followed Him because He gave them something to drink. But they did not join themselves to the true Vine. They followed Him only because they wanted to receive something from Him. And the Lord, who is so good and whose mercies endure forever, gave them healing. But what did the nine lepers do? They came near the Vine, the Lord healed them, and what did they do? Nine lepers walked away from the Vine. I don’t know if that happens nowadays; I don’t know. And if it does happen, the Lord Jesus Christ wants to give you your miracle and heal you. He wants to restore your life and your marriage, and give you a different life. But He doesn’t only want to give you those things. He wants you to join yourself to the true Vine, because if you do, you will remain with Him for eternity. He doesn’t want to give you something temporary, just for a little while; He wants to give you everything. Amen. Glory to God.

John 15:6: What Happens When Trials Come

John 15:6 says:

“If anyone does not remain in me, he is thrown away like a branch and withers; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they burn.”

Let me explain this verse in a different way. Maybe when you see this, you’ll say, “No, I already knew that.” But the Lord has just shown it to me. Amen. The Bible says that those of us who are separated from the Vine will wither, and the fire will come and we will burn. Usually, this verse is used to illustrate something else, but this is how the Lord showed it to me. Maybe He showed it to some of you differently, or the same way—I don’t know—but I’m going to use this. I was going to buy a leaf blower, but I didn’t have enough in my budget, so I made a Mexican contraption. This is a little pump for blowing air into a mattress, and this is a little hose I bought for putting oil in the car. I stuck it on here because, well, I didn’t have enough money.

Let me explain it another way. The Bible says that those of us who separate ourselves from the Vine will wither and burn in the fire. That means that when problems come, this will happen. (He pulls a little—better if I do it with my foot.) When weariness comes, this will happen. When discouragement comes, this will happen. When sickness comes, this will happen. When trials come, this will happen. And do you know why that will happen? Because we were only near the Vine; we were not in the true Vine. Glory to God.

But those of us who remain in the true Vine, who are joined to the true Vine, brothers and sisters, may face problems, sickness, trials, weariness, and discouragement. What’s more, you may hear a comment from a leader or a brother or sister, and it won’t affect you. On the contrary, you will keep bearing fruit—and even more fruit—because you are joined to the true Vine. You will remain in the true Vine no matter what happens or what people say to you.

When we are not joined to the true Vine, we hear something about someone and want to leave; we want to distance ourselves from the church. Someone says something to us, we hear something, a trial comes, and we even want to stop serving the Lord Jesus Christ. These here serve God too, but when trials, discouragement, or weariness come, they no longer want to serve God. But if you remain in the true Vine, people can say whatever they want to you, and you will keep serving the Lord Jesus Christ, because you are joined to the One who bears fruit. He is the true Vine, and you and I are the branches.

The Lord Jesus Christ, brothers and sisters, is the sustenance you and I need. Just as you and I need air, water, and food to stay alive here on earth, we need the Lord Jesus Christ in our lives. We need to be joined to Him so that we won’t wither. He is our sustenance; everything comes from Him, everything we have comes from Him.

And remaining in, or being joined to, the true Vine does not mean that you and I live doing whatever we want. It doesn’t mean that we live our own way. Rather, if we are joined to the true Vine, we must live according to the will of the Lord Jesus Christ, doing and accepting His will, living as He wants us to live. That is what it means to live in and remain in the true Vine.

Brother, I say this with all due respect, and the Lord put it on my heart. We cannot think that we are in the true Vine while we keep lying, while there is deceit in us, while we are envious or proud, while we refuse to forgive or submit to those in authority, while there are quarrels among brothers and sisters, or while we keep using bad language. We cannot keep saying or thinking that we are joined to the Vine if we are still doing these things. We cannot say we are joined to the true Vine if we hear something or someone says something to us and the first thing we say is, “I’m not going to church anymore.” We cannot say we are joined to the Vine if we go through a trial and want to leave the ministry, if we no longer want to serve the Lord Jesus Christ. We cannot say we are joined to the Vine if we still have those thoughts.

Pruning

We must remain in the true Vine at all times so that we can bear fruit, and much fruit, and bear quality fruit—excellent fruit. The Bible tells us that we must be pruned, that we must be cleansed, so that we can bear much fruit. John 15:2:

“Every branch in me that does not bear fruit, he takes away; and every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, so that it may bear more fruit.”

Glory to God. As I was studying this sermon, the Lord spoke to me so much. I asked the Lord a question: “Why are there so many of us who are in the Vine and bear fruit, but then fall away? Why do we wither after a while?” And the Lord told me, “It’s because pruning is not pleasant for many people. Not everyone can endure being pruned. That is why they fall away, and that is why they wither.” And the Lord said, “But tell them that my purpose, my intention in pruning and cleansing them, is not to hurt them, not to make them fall away, wither, or distance themselves from me. It is so that they will bear much fruit.” Pruning is not easy, brothers and sisters. Pruning is hard.

I said to Him, “Explain it to me differently, because I didn’t go to school. I understand, but explain it to me in a simpler way.” And He said, “Okay, let’s say you have some fruit, but you tell me, ‘Oh Lord, give me patience. I want patience.’” Brother, pruning means He may send someone to test your patience, someone who will make you want to strangle them. Then you say, “Oh Lord, give me faith. I want to have faith in you, Lord.” Brother, pruning: the Lord may send you sickness, and, even harder, He may let your child become sick. And the Lord said, “That is why so many cannot endure my pruning: I want to give them much more fruit. That is why they separate themselves from me. They already had some fruit, but because they cannot endure the pruning, they spoil the fruit they already had, fall away and wither, and want to abandon me.” But God’s intention is to cleanse us.

Brothers and sisters, pruning is so important. I studied this: if you don’t prune a vine, it won’t bear fruit. There are trees you prune so they will grow and provide shade, but vines, brothers and sisters, are pruned so they will bear fruit. That is why it is so important: through this sermon, the Lord wants you to endure the pruning, to endure the cleansing, because in the end He will give you much more fruit, until your vine is full of all the fruit of the Holy Spirit. Glory to His name.

Close to Him, but Not Joined to Him

And the Lord also told me—I’ve already explained this—He said, “There are people who have been in the church for years. There are people who have spent a long time in a life group. There are people who attend the service every Sunday.” The Lord told me, “But they are only near me, only near me; they don’t want to join themselves to me.” And that happens to many people. Several people have told me in our life groups that they like coming to church, that they feel good here, and that they like having us visit them. But when you talk to them about commitment, when you talk to them about baptism—which is one way of joining yourself to the Vine—that’s where it all ends. What the Lord wants, brother, friend, is for you not merely to be around Him, not merely to be near Him, but to join yourself to Him. You can be near Him and settle for that, but that is not what matters most. What matters is being in Him, being in the true Vine, and holding on to Him through whatever you struggle with, because He will cleanse you and help you. He doesn’t want only to give you a miracle, brother; He doesn’t want only to heal you, restore your marriage, make you a deacon, or make you a minister. He wants to give salvation to you, to your life, and to your family.

I’m almost finished. John 15:5 says:

“I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever remains in me, and I in him, bears much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing.”

Brothers and sisters, what a powerful word the Lord Jesus is speaking to us here: God in us, and us in God. That is why it is so important to remain joined to God, because it also says here that without Him, apart from Him, we can do nothing. Perhaps you are striving and trying to bear fruit, but you are not joined to Him. Today is the day to reflect and consider whether you are truly joined to the true Vine, or merely near the true Vine.

The Gardener

In these verses, the Lord says that His Father is the gardener. Brothers and sisters, the gardener is the one who tends the vineyard: caring for it, protecting it, pruning it, and cleaning it so it will grow stronger and bear more fruit. The gardener cares for the vineyard when it becomes diseased, because plants get sick too. He is the one who tends it and gives it water so it can keep bearing fruit. The gardener removes every dry branch from the vine and every dry leaf, so they won’t ruin the whole vineyard. That is why, at times, the Lord Jesus will remove people from your circle for a while—only for a while—because He knows they could ruin you if He leaves them there. But once He has made you, the branch, stronger, He will bring those dry branches back to you, so you can speak to them and preach that He is the true Vine, and that He is the one who gives us the fruit of the Holy Spirit.

Today our heavenly Father, who is the Gardener, is here. The true Vine, the Lord Jesus Christ, is here today. This is just something I used as an illustration, as He also used an illustration back then, but He is the true Vine. The Lord Jesus Christ is our true Vine, and through the Scriptures, through the Bible, God teaches us all what should happen when you and I are born again. He teaches us how important it is to grow spiritually and to remain joined to the true Vine. But the Lord teaches us that it is not enough just to be around Him. He teaches us that when we are joined to the true Vine, the Holy Spirit produces fruit in us—in our lives and in our hearts. He teaches us to seek the presence of the Holy Spirit in prayer, because the Holy Spirit is the one who will give us this fruit.

We Were Chosen to Bear Fruit

The Word of God says in the book of John that you and I did not choose the Lord Jesus Christ. You and I did not choose God. Perhaps God wasn’t on your agenda; perhaps He wasn’t in your plans. But you were on God’s agenda and in His plans. Amen. It says that He chose us, and He did not choose us just to come to church every Sunday. He did not choose us just so you and your family would be well, or so things would go well for you financially. He did not choose you to give you a house or to make you a servant or a leader. All of that is important; it comes later. Do you know why He chose you and me? Do you know why? So that we would bear fruit. That is why He chose us. That is why He took notice of you and me: to bear fruit, and much fruit. Look at John 15:16:

“You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you to go and bear fruit, fruit that will remain, so that whatever you ask of the Father in my name, he may give it to you.”

That is why the Lord Jesus Christ chose us. He wants us to bear fruit, and much fruit. And it says, “and that your fruit will remain.” Brother, that tells me we can bear fruit and then lose it later. Because God says, “I chose you to bear fruit, but for that fruit to remain forever.” Glory to God.

Let me read you the verse we read at the beginning, John 15:5, in a more modern version, because perhaps you’re wondering, “What is a branch?” Let’s see. The Lord says, “I am the vine; you are the branches.” All these are branches; I am the vine. All this brown part you see is like the tree. He says, “You are the branches. Whoever remains in me, as I remain in him, will bear much fruit. But the branch that does not remain in me, in the vine, will not be able to bear fruit for me.” Then He finishes by saying, “Because apart from me, you yourselves will not produce any fruit; you cannot produce any fruit apart from me.” And if the Word of God says that we cannot bear fruit apart from Him, it’s because we cannot bear fruit. That’s another version. That is why it is so important for every branch to remain joined to the vine so it can bear much fruit.

Altar Call

Please stand, brother. The Lord Jesus Christ is very clear in these words. Crystal clear, brother—He couldn’t be clearer; as they say, clearer than water. To bear fruit, we must depend 100 percent on the true Vine; we must depend 100 percent on the Holy Spirit. We must seek the Holy Spirit more, because the Holy Spirit is the one who will help us bear much fruit and remain joined to the true Vine. Brothers and sisters, remaining joined to the true Vine—that is what the Lord Jesus Christ wants us all to do. That is His desire, that is His will.

Today the Lord came to stir some of us, to give us a shake-up, turn us upside down—whatever you want to call it—but not to harm us, because He wants us, brother, to depend on Him 100 percent. That is what He desires, and that is what He wanted to teach us today: that you and I were chosen to bear fruit for Him—not just one fruit, but many fruits, many fruits—so that He may be glorified through us.

Brother, I want to open this altar to everyone, not just some people and not others. Everyone who wants to come and bow before the true Vine and ask the Lord Jesus Christ for strength to stay joined to the true Vine. I believe we are all joined to the true Vine, but we need His strength to keep going like this for all eternity, to keep bearing fruit for Him. Brothers and sisters, I invite you to come, bow down, and tell the Lord Jesus Christ that He is worthy, that He is worthy of our worship, our praise, and our gratitude; that we give Him glory and honor. Thank You, thank You for keeping us joined to the true Vine.

Paul says in 2 Corinthians, chapter 5, verse 17:

“Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; the old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new.”

The old fruits have passed away; behold, all the fruits are new. Glory to God. That is what the Lord Jesus Christ’s cleansing is for; that is what pruning is for: to give us new fruit. That is what the Gardener does. And the Gardener, the one who tends the vineyard, is here today. I can’t know whether you need to be pruned today; I don’t know. I don’t know why the Lord gave me this Word. I don’t know whether you need to be cleansed; I don’t know. But if this Word touched your heart, come here. Come, for the true Vine is here. If you also want to stay joined to the true Vine, come here and ask the Lord for strength.

Brothers and sisters, the Bible tells me that we can have much fruit, and then at some point have none at all. So it is very important to ask Him for strength, so that we may remain with this fruit—and bear even more—because our strength comes from Him. He gives us the fruit of the Holy Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, faith, gentleness, and self-control. These are all the fruits the Lord Jesus Christ wants us to have; He wants us to bear all these fruits. Come, brother, come. Tell Him that He is worthy. Come, tell Him that He deserves the glory and the honor.
