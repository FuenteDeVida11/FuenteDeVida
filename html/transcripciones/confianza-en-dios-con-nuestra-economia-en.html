Introduction

Amen. Glory to the Lord. Are you doing well, church? You look like you could use a little coffee.

Dear friends and brothers and sisters in Christ, I am going to address a very important subject in a Christian's life, and one that we have not talked about much at Fuente de Vida. We have never dedicated a sermon to it. A few weeks ago, I discussed it with the cell-group leaders, because we need to speak to the leaders first, and then we will all do our homework. The subject is tithing. You can visit Fuente de Vida's Facebook and YouTube pages, and you will see that among all the sermons posted, you will not find one about tithing. Why? Because we have a problem: for many people, "church" is synonymous with taking money, with churches profiting from people, and there is tremendous confusion. So today we are committed to establishing the biblical standard, what God says.

There are two reasons for preaching this.

Reason number one. The Lord had been troubling my heart, because this was a struggle I had. I used to say, "I am not going to talk about money, Lord, because what matters to You is souls. Help me, because I don't want people to come in here and think that, because that's what happens in other churches." But the Holy Spirit had been convicting me for some time—about three years—because I am very disobedient. You are holy, but I am disobedient. And God would tell me, "Let them trust Me the way you do. Do you trust Me with the tithe? Try trusting Me. Do you entrust to God the same level of confidence you have in your finances?" So this is the time to talk about the church also entrusting its finances to God, because those who trust God are protected and blessed by God. Our blessing comes from the trust we place in Him. And God told me, "Don't stop there. You have to teach the principles of tithes and offerings, and you are not teaching them." When the boss speaks, you have to listen. I had been like this for three years, telling the Lord, "If You want me to talk about this, give me the grace, because people love money so much."

And I said, people are incredible. They can cry and give one dollar as an offering at church, but they will pay thousands of dollars to immigration lawyers. The amens are gone; the amens in Christ are gone. They will pay for VIP tables at dances, give money for the front table, the amens are gone; they will throw money around, but they are afraid to give at church. Am I right? Are you okay? Yes. There are brothers who have been giving the same five dollars as an offering since 1998. It is the same five dollars. It has become repetitive. They even look around for the five dollars they have for the offering. The same thing, over and over.

Reason number two. Fuente de Vida needs another building, and God can do great miracles, just as He did with this place. We did not come into this place because we had money, brothers and sisters. We have access to the whole building; everything is here to serve the Lord. We had nothing. God opened doors, and we moved in with only a group of about one hundred brothers and sisters. But if we bring the two congregations together, there is no room for all of us. For Friend Day, we have to rent gyms; we cannot bring the church together for one service. Parking is a problem. I have a brother who does not want to get baptized because if he does, he will have to park outside. Parking is a problem in our congregation. We need a new home. And I am embarrassed to say this, but we do not have the finances because most of us do not tithe; we do not trust God with them.

Now you understand the background of my sermon. God will do miracles, but we must not be disobedient either. With all due respect, the government is not going to give us a building. And even if it did, maintaining a building for the size we need costs thousands of dollars a month. Then God told me, "Do what you have to do." If God said it, brother, get moving. If you are a woman, sister, get moving.

Sister, this sermon is about trusting God with our finances.

What Does It Mean to Trust?

When we talk about trust, it means I no longer do things my own way. I do them the way God established. That is trust. I cannot say, "I trust," and then keep the door closed. Leave it open if you trust. Trust is shown by doing things the way God said.

For example, I no longer manage my marriage however I want; I do it the way God requires. If I have a marriage and I am going to entrust it to God, what am I going to do with it? I will no longer be jealous. I will trust as God told me to. I will not be angry with her all the time, offend her, or refuse to make lunch for my husband. That comes from God. So I am no longer doing it my way; I am surrendering it to God. I will do what God requires. What does God say? Love your wife as Christ loved His church. And the Bible tells you how to treat your husband. That is how I show that I trust God. I cannot say, "I entrust my marriage to God," and still act spoiled, rude, and dishonest. That is not trusting God with my marriage. The police are going to be called, and they will ask, "What is going on at Fuente de Vida?"

It is the same with our youth. I give my youth to God. I cannot tell God, "I give You my youth," and then go to dances and drink, but not go to church. I will serve You, Lord, and give You my life, my days, and my fasting. I will make decisions in my youth based on Your Word. I entrusted my youth, my decisions, and whom I will marry to God. That is what my trust in Him is about.

We entrust our children to the Lord. "I entrust my children to God," but then how can I take them to play soccer during the church service? I entrust my children to God, but I tell them, "Go, study, and make lots of money," even if God is calling them to serve Him. When I entrust them to Him, I say, "Son, go to college, but I will pray for you, because your decision has to do with a calling God has for you. Remember the Lord when you are there." I am entrusting my children to God by giving them the Word of God. That is what entrusting them means.

It is about trusting. I will entrust my ministry to the Lord. Some of us say, "I am going to entrust my ministry to God." But people come and say, "Oh, you sing so beautifully." They invite you to sing at a church, and you say, "No, that church is too small." They only want to sing at conventions. They do not like going to sing elsewhere. But why not? If your ministry belongs to the Lord, you gave it to the Lord—or do you want your ministry for yourself? You can choose not to entrust your ministry to the Lord, and you will end up in ruin. Or you can choose to do what you want with it, or give your ministry to the Lord.

Brothers and sisters, when we get to tithing, when we get to finances, we say, "God, I entrust my children to You, my youth to You, my ministry to You," but there is no offering and no tithe. Your ministry is fully entrusted, but you have not entrusted your finances to God. The only thing that will happen is that you will not receive the blessing that comes from trusting God with your finances. You will have a nice guitar, but struggle to find work.

It is very important to understand: tithing is not mandatory. The tithe is the way God teaches us to trust Him. Beloved church, the list can grow: entrust your health to God, entrust everything to God. What do you entrust to the Lord? Trust means doing things God's way, not mine. Tithing is not mandatory under the New Testament, but it is a way God uses to help us understand whether we trust Him or not.

Peace of Christ. I have to speak to you about this subject, because people love money, and money can bring a person's downfall. A man or woman who trusts God prays like this: "Do not give me too much, because I may forget You. I prefer Your presence and Your direction. An abundance of money could be my downfall. But do not give me too little, either, or I will curse Your name, Lord. I need You to care for me and help me with my finances."

This matter of money is a problem. Many times I have remembered an illustration Brother Abel once told us. There was a shepherd shearing the sheep, taking their wool. He was old and had trouble seeing, so he felt around for them. Then a pig crossed his path. He grabbed it and started shearing it, and the pig squealed, as pigs do. He said, "So much squealing and so little wool." There are brothers and sisters who squeal at church: they have not given a single cent, they talk about tithing, and give nothing. "Don't offend me; let me give if I trust God. It's not even your money." They even argue about it online. I would not be surprised if someone puts on their boxing gloves online and attacks me over tithing, because people start fighting. This subject is about trust.

God has ways of guiding us to success, just as He does in marriage and in raising our children. He also has a way to teach us to trust Him with a good financial life and the riches He gives. God can give them, but it has to do with the heart. God's riches are for us.

Matthew 6:19-21

When we talk about money, you know it has to do with what is in a person's heart. Matthew 6, verses 19 through 21:

"Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal; but store up for yourselves treasures in heaven... For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."

People love money as if money were everything. For many people, money is everything. That is not true: there are riches greater than money. Peace: you can buy a bed, but you cannot buy sleep. You can buy a house, but not a home. I could keep going, brothers and sisters. Money is not everything, but people get tangled up in it because of what is in their hearts.

We Are Stewards

Do you know that we are only stewards of the riches God has given us? Everything we have comes from God. Your beauty and intelligence were given to you by God. We are stewards of what God gave us. Every skill you have—how you play guitar, how you know how to do what you do—and your intelligence were given to you by God. Your family and your children were given to you by God. We are only managers of what He gave us.

You have heard the parable of the talents, and you realize that God does not give the same amount to everyone. He gave one person five talents and another one talent, and God does not come to demand five from you. God only asks you to account for what He gave you, because you are supposed to work with what you have, for your benefit and your own reward. This is about managing the riches God gives us. God will give more to the one who manages well. He multiplied what the person with five talents had; He took from the one who had none and gave it to him. Your poverty and your attitude do not come from nowhere. They come from poor management.

The Story of the Chickens

I want you to watch a video. This video will help us see how God blesses us in mysterious ways when we trust Him with our finances, because our blessing comes from Him.

This preacher tells this story: "I asked my grandmother, 'What is the greatest miracle you have seen in fifty years of preaching?' This may seem like a small miracle to many, but it was great for us. She said, 'I woke up one morning and we had no money. We were preaching on the street, at a Bible college, trusting God. We woke up and there was no food for breakfast. I was ready to pray, and suddenly, before I could pray, I saw chickens coming down the road. We lived in West Virginia. They came all the way to our house—21 chickens. Chickens normally do not roam around; they keep them in a pen. We watched them come down the road in a line and onto the property. I went out to see what they were doing. All 21 chickens laid an egg. I gathered the eggs and chased the chickens back half a mile to where they had come from. My grandmother, being honest, knocked on the door. The woman opened it, and my grandmother said, "Your chickens got loose and came onto my property." She said, "Those cannot be my chickens. My husband hates those chickens. Ever since we bought them, they have never laid an egg." My grandmother said, "We don't have any chickens." And she said, "I promise you, they are yours. If they are on your property, keep them." From then on, my grandparents lived there. Those chickens came down to the property every day, laid eggs, and left. My grandmother never had to clean up after them, feed them, or buy a pen for them. She just received the blessing.' And I tell you, in the name of Jesus, if all you have done is struggle, the problem will not find you; God's blessing will come without your permission. You only have to receive it."

Amazing. When you trust God with your finances, you are telling Him, "I have this job, but You are truly my provider. If I do not have a job, You will provide for me." Trusting God with our finances. I have many examples, but I wanted you to hear someone else's, because I am not here to convince you. I am here to preach the method, the system God uses to bless His church. He will give to you if you trust Him. He will give to you if you work according to the method, the way He wants you to work. Why does God work this way? Because tithing is a way of learning to trust.

Is Tithing in the New Testament?

Someone said tithing is not in the New Testament. They quickly say, "Give me one verse in the New Testament that talks about tithing." Matthew 23:23. Memorize it:

"Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint, dill, and cumin, and have neglected the more important matters of the law: justice, mercy, and faith. These you ought to have done, without neglecting the others."

What was Israel's problem? They were tithing and counting everything precisely, and they thought they were good because they tithed. They trusted in the tithe, but not in God, and they forgot to show mercy to those in need. He tells them, "These you ought to have done": tithe without neglecting the other things, because the most important part of the tithe is its essence: trusting God, doing good to others, helping, and blessing. The tithe is not so you can become rich. It is so you can bless those who are in need. Even if you do not like hearing it, brother, the money is not for you. The money is to serve the Lord, and the Lord knows how to care for His servants. The Lord knows how to bless you.

Today, someone may receive this Word: tithing has not ended. It is something God wants today as well.

Proverbs 26:2

"Like a sparrow in its flitting, like a swallow in its flying, a curse that is causeless does not alight."

Brother, you may be going through a process, but poverty is not for you today. You are a child of God. God knows how to care for His children; He knows how to feed the birds. "Pastor, you look like you are just standing there for decoration today." Do you know I once threw myself into a trash can while praying to God? I am not here to convince you. If you do not want to, that is fine; I am sharing what He gave me, which is to trust God.

It is not just about giving money. That is what the Jews did: they tithed by force, saying, "You have to bless me, God," or they tithed without believing. What God gives you is for helping others. I am at peace. A curse does not come without a cause; it has a cause. I am not going to say God gave you five talents if you can only handle one. Wealth and blessing do not just appear for no reason, and neither does a curse. You have to look at where you are financially and how much you trust the Lord. Peace of Christ.

The Greedy

We must be good stewards. Do you want me to keep going? You will not like what I am about to say, but here it is: the stingy people of Monterrey—I do not know what they call them in Venezuela—people who do not want to buy anything, who will not buy a banana because they do not want to throw away the peel. There are people like that in church, brothers and sisters, who do not see the vision of God's Kingdom. They think we can pay the electricity bill with four amens and two hallelujahs. When we build the new parking lot, are we going to stop and fast for five days to see if that builds the parking lot? No. God asks us to trust Him.

In 2003, if I am not mistaken, the recession came to this country. There was absolutely no work. My boss began telling us there was no work. I do not know if we were going to close our doors; several companies closed. I told him, "Let's pray for our finances. God will keep us." And I prayed for our company. That was more than twenty years ago, and the company is still here. He told me, "Pray for us, because we do not trust what comes from outside; we trust that God will sustain us." God has sustained our company to this day because we trusted in the Lord.

So it is very important to understand: if you want to fight, go ahead and fight by yourself if you want, but the greedy are on the list. The greedy are on the list of those who will not inherit the kingdom of heaven. "Get mad at me." You do not have to tithe. I do not see your tithes, brothers and sisters; God is my witness. The treasurers do the administrative work, and may God reward them, because the church does not pay them a salary. They give their time so that we can have the electricity and water paid and meet the requirements of the city. May God bless the church administrators, who serve the Lord with love and donate their time and blessings to our congregation. But this is not about convincing you. Keep coming to Fuente de Vida; erase this sermon from your mind if you want. I do not want anything to get in the way of your seeking God. But I will tell a young person today: dare to entrust your finances to the Lord.

Let's look at that list. First Corinthians 6:9-11 (it is also in Revelation, but I will give you this one):

"Do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor men who practice homosexuality, nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God."

Go ahead and get mad. But anyway, I am talking about trusting God.

My Testimony

I was baptized on August 5, 2000, and I have entrusted my finances to the Lord. I have tithed since 2000. I began pastoring ten years ago, and the Lord has given me many blessings. The Lord has worked in my life and blessed me in many ways. Sometimes I have gone through difficult financial situations, and I believe the only reason God allows me to go through them is to remind me that my finances do not come from the company where I work or from my own strength, but from Him. Get ready for this: the system you have here will not serve you for eternity, because in heaven you will depend completely on God. You will no longer depend on a job or on yourself, because the Lord will provide everything. Could God be training us here? Amen.

Brother, I have had difficult financial situations. Yes, I have, but it was when I did not trust the Lord, when I said, "Do I really have to give Him some?" until I said, "No, this belongs to the Lord." And that was that. So I tithe to the district; I do not give to the local church. I am required to tithe to my leadership, and the bishop always tells us, "How can you dare tell people to trust God if you do not trust God yourself?" So we need to be ethical.

You are at the cell group saying, "God, You are going to work; God, You are going to do it in a way I do not know." And then this happens. Hold on, because this part is going downhill. Brothers, God will bless you richly, but He will not bless you while you sit in your chair. You have to go and seek your blessing.

I remember arriving in this state broke, brother, without money. I had a truck with no heat. I would take a pencil and do this so people would think I was smoking, because I would breathe a little fog onto the windshield; otherwise, since I did not know English, I could not see. I had been baptized for two years, and I went through hardship at that time. So I do not know what you have, God, but I trust You; I never lacked a meal on the table. God always provided for me. I said, "Lord, I cannot just keep waiting here. I have prayed; I have cried out to You." I told a brother from church, "Take me to a construction site. I am going to find my blessing." At the first place I stopped, the Lord... I am not lying to you; that is where I met Brother Iván. I got there and said, "Look..." They needed someone to work, and the next day I started working. I do not believe in coincidences; I believe in "God-incidences." The Lord shows the way to those who are willing to trust Him. God places us and blesses us. If He does not give you the money, He can lower the price of the house so you can buy it. He will put you where you can trust Him. Here is a good one: if you do not trust the Lord and go where you should not, the blessing you have will become a curse. Do not force things; do not lie. Do things the right way, and the Lord will bless you. He will do it.

So the greedy are on this list. I have a brother—I will not say his name so he cannot find himself here—but he gets upset when I talk about finances. Yet I never see him dancing when we baptize young people for the glory of the Lord. Then I realize that the problem is not finances; it is his heart. May God forgive him. Thank you for that amen; God forgave him just for that amen. This is about trusting the Lord. How many of you trust the Lord?

Malachi 3:8-12

I am going to read my last passage, and of course it is the passage I like most when it talks about tithing, because it contains a great teaching. Malachi 3, verses 8 through 12:

"Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me. But you say, 'How have we robbed You?' In your tithes and offerings. You are cursed with a curse, for you are robbing Me, the whole nation of you. Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in My house. And thereby put Me to the test, says the Lord of hosts, if I will not open the windows of heaven for you and pour down for you a blessing until there is no more need. I will rebuke the devourer for you, so that it will not destroy the fruits of your soil, and your vine in the field shall not fail to bear, says the Lord of hosts. Then all nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land of delight, says the Lord of hosts."

I believe this passage explains itself, but there is a great revelation. Look at how this works. It is not, "I give You a tithe, God, and if I give You 100, I know You will give me 1,000." That is not how it works. Look at verse 8: "Will a man rob God?" It says, "I am cursed because you have robbed Me." The point is to repent and tell the Lord, "It is true, I have not entrusted my finances to You, because I claim to trust You, but I am not trusting You." Look at verse 10: "Bring the full tithe into the storehouse." What will happen? "That there may be food in My house." Then He says, "Test Me in this." And He says, "I will open the windows of heaven." It does not literally mean a new job will fall from the sky. I do not know what you need, but it will not fall that way. What are windows for? To let in the light, the sun. In other words, God will enlighten you about where you are spending your money, how you are spending it, and where you should invest it. God will give you the ability to understand.

You may say, "That is not true." I have a house, and I do not want to give you the key because you might not return it. I built it with my family. No one believed I would have a house; I am serious. Carla and I built it, and she was pregnant with Jacob. I remember my cousin walked all around the house and said, "Let's see when the gringo comes out," because he thought I was renting it. It is not mine—well, it belongs to the bank, but it is mine too. God will open heaven's windows and show you where to spend your money. God will tell you, "Not here; here."

Then He says He will rebuke the devourer for us, the one who is eating up your blessing. My money is no longer taken by alcohol or drugs. You laugh, but there are people suffering right now because they cannot pay rent, because beer comes first, and the devourer, the enemy, is robbing them. But the Lord rebuked my devourer and will protect the fruit I sow. Everything I have will produce and will not be barren. Whatever I do will be from God and blessed. The doors of my blessing will not be shut; they will be on my side, because a curse does not come without a cause.

I have experienced all kinds of things. I can tell you about people who have not paid me for my labor. I will leave it there for you to see: they have not paid, but do not hold it against them. God will take care of it. I am not telling you this to convince you; this is how the Lord works. God does not keep what is not His. Time passes, brother, and then the person comes and says, "Do you remember that you lent me this much? I was not even asking you to pay me back." "I could not sleep." The prayer of a righteous person is powerful.

"All nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land of delight, says the Lord." For someone, this sermon is a reminder, because your mother tithed, your father tithed, and today the Lord is telling you, "Trust Me. Trust Me with your finances."

I have told the Lord... I remember a time when I was going through a difficult period. You do not know where I come from. My family comes from a very idolatrous background. I do not know if you know this, but in religions they sell you things that supposedly bless you. Instead of following God's plan, they sold supposedly blessed soil. I remember a difficult time financially, and I said to God... but my spirit rebuked me and said, "Will you accept only good days from God and not bad days?" And I remembered and said, "When I did not know You, I ate dirt, and I endured eating dirt out there in the world. How can I not endure, Lord?" I do not know what You have for me. I do not have enough; I am not rich. I do not need to be rich. I am happy with what I have. I do not need more; I do not want more. Maybe it would draw me away from God. I entrust my finances to God.

The Offering: A Moment of Trust

That is why I told the brothers we are not going to collect the offering, in case some greedy person here said, "Oh, there was no offering." We want to use the offering time not to collect more money from you, but as the moment when we tell the Lord, "Just as I entrust my trial to You, my marriage to You, and my finances to You, whether I bring three dollars or three pesos, Lord, I trust that my provision comes from You," so that we can see financial miracles in our congregation. I cannot watch you suffer and struggle to pay rent. God can provide you with a steady job so you will never have to ask anyone else for help.

I will have worked at my company for 25 years, and I have never lacked work, because the Lord has been there. God has given me favor at work. "Send Jacobo; let him come work here. I do not want anyone else; I want him." That comes from God. God taught me how to do my work, how to go about it, and how to deal with people. It does not mean thousands of dollars will suddenly appear in your account. They do appear. Glory to God.

Let us not make the offering... let us make the offering a moment of trust. I know this is not for everyone. That is what Jacob did: "Of all that You give me, I will surely give the tenth to You." He is not asking you for what you do not have; he is saying what Jacob said: the tithe.

Oh, my brother, if you only knew. Through this sermon, the Lord will not be limited in blessing you in some way. God will provide scholarships for your children. May the Lord give you a house so you no longer have to pay rent. I am not selling you a house; I am declaring a great blessing over you. Stop struggling to see if you can come up with the rent. May the Lord... I come from where you come from. Maybe you have more education than I do; maybe your family is better than mine. Water used to come into the cardboard house where I lived while I was trying to finish paying for the land. I trusted the Lord. Trust the Lord with your finances.

Someone said, "They do not need the tithe here." No. Give it because of the commitment you made to the Lord, the agreement you made with me—with God. I will not forget a sister, Sister Blandón, who worked at a place where they would not let her arrive early at church. She would run to the service, and the offering had already been collected; the brothers were no longer there. I do not know if any of you remember: she would come and leave her tithe here. During the pandemic, brothers and sisters, she called: "Pastor, come by the house; we are sick with COVID and cannot go out." "Sister, we are busy too." "No, Pastor, I am not going out; I just have something for you." I did not know she passed her tithe to me down below. "Sister, do you need anything else, Pastor?" "I do not have this commitment with you; my commitment is with God." She would always come and leave her tithe here, and they had to pick it up later. That was her trust in the Lord. When she arrived here, I prayed for a van for her; she bought one from Brother Freddy. That van never gave her trouble the entire time she had it, until she returned to Nicaragua. She later had to give it to her son, because it never... it was tremendous faith. Her husband did not come to church and did not want anything to do with church, but he understood something: "You have a commitment to God, woman," because he knew God answered through her finances and through her trust.

Invitation

I want to invite you, starting now... even if it is one dollar. It is an offering, and you give it with love to the Lord. Even if it is one dollar: "Thank You, Lord, for what You gave me." This is a moment to give an offering to the Lord, when I tell Him, "Here is my trust. Thank You, Lord; I depend on You for my finances." And I place them in His hands with trust. I am not scolding you. Is that okay? It feels like a scolding, right? No. Okay, I do not want this to sound like a scolding; it is an explanation. "I entrust this to You, Lord."

I feel the Lord is stirring something, brother. Trust the Lord with your finances. One day you will not have the strength you have today; one day you will not have the health you have today. But if you trust the Lord now, He will not abandon you. Even when you are no longer in that condition, the Lord will provide for you, because you are His child. So trust Him today; do not be greedy in the presence of God. Give your offering to the Lord with love. Give it to Him in trust.

Is there someone here who can entrust their finances to the Lord right where you are? Today, I invite you, if you want a time of prayer, to say, "Lord, I need a breakthrough in my finances.
