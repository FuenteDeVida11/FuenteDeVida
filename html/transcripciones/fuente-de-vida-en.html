Sermon transcript

Full transcript of the message preached by Bishop Zúñiga.

Sermon transcript. Bible verses are enclosed in quotation marks.

Key Scripture: Romans 8:28

Romans, chapter 8, verse 28. This verse has been a great blessing in my life because it helps me understand many things. Give praise to the Lord.

"And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose."

Brothers and sisters, if you listen to this message, God will bless you. But we have to understand and know the Word of God. This is what the Lord wants to teach us so we can live a victorious life here on earth. How many have victory in Christ?

The apostle Paul says, "We know that all things work together for good to those who love God..." That is a key to the Christian life: loving God. The Christian life is very difficult, but if you love God, you can endure anything. And it does not say some things; it says all things. Someone say, "They work together for good." Those who are called according to His purpose. Tell the person next to you, "God called you for a purpose." God has a purpose for you, and it is an eternal purpose. Glory to God.

Dedicated to Those Who Have Been Through Trials

I want to dedicate this message to this church, and also to the brothers and sisters who have gone through trials and struggles but are still here by God's grace. Raise your hand if you have been through trials and struggles. God bless those who did not raise their hands. I want to dedicate this message to the brothers and sisters who have faced trials and struggles and come through victorious. How many of you are here? Raise your hands to heaven. Tell Him, "Lord, speak to me." Not to your neighbor—to you. "Lord, speak to me. I need to hear Your Word so it can build me up, and so I can leave this service a victor." You can see defeat on a person's face, and you can see victory on a person's face.

You may sit down, but do not stop praising the Lord. Who am I dedicating this message to? Everyone who has faced trials and struggles and is still here. I believe we have all gone through trials and struggles, but we have to make a distinction. One thing is to face trials because of our foolishness and mistakes; it is very different to face trials because we serve Christ. Do you understand, brothers and sisters? I am speaking to those who have become involved in a spiritual battle because they serve Christ. If there is someone who does not want you to fulfill God's purpose, he will attack you in many ways to discourage you and keep you from serving God.

But I have learned that when you have a purpose from God, no matter what is thrown at us, God will fulfill that purpose in us. In fact, trials and struggles help us fulfill that purpose. Hallelujah. Sometimes, brothers and sisters, if the Lord lets us go without trials, we tend to forget Him. But God puts us between a rock and a hard place so we will say, "Only God can get me out of this." That is when you give God the glory. How many has the Lord brought out of trials that only He could bring you through?

I have noticed that the brothers and sisters who say, "No, I have it all together; nothing has happened to me," offer very little praise, because they feel they have no reason to glorify the Lord. But I have also seen brothers and sisters go through trials where the enemy attacked their health, finances, marriage, even their car, and God brought them through. Their praise is more intense, because when they look back they have to say, "If it had not been for the Lord, I would not be here this morning."

I want to take a minute for those who have faced trials and struggles, may have been knocked down, but are still standing, and can say, "I can still praise God as if nothing had happened." Someone praise His name!

The trials God allows are not meant to destroy you; they are meant to build you up and help you know God better. It is easy to praise God when everything is going well. But what happens when things do not go the way we want? Will we stop praising God? We can praise God even in trials and struggles.

If I had written what James wrote, I would have said something different: "Count it all joy when you receive many blessings." But James says the opposite:

"My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials."

How does that work? In the middle of your trial, you can praise God. The enemy says, "This person is going to be quiet today; they will not open their mouth to praise God." But you can praise God in the middle of the trial. That confuses the enemy. This church has been through trials and struggles; the enemy has thrown this and that at you, but he has not been able to silence you. Instead, you praise the Lord even more. I want to know how many can praise the Lord even more. Where are those courageous people? Someone say, "Glory to God." Tell the person next to you, "Never, ever stop praising God, whatever the circumstances. Never stop praising God."

An Eternal Purpose

Let's keep going. You have to understand, brother, church, that God chose you and this church to fulfill an eternal purpose. As a human being, I can fail at some plans; I have many plans. But I do not want to fail in God's eternal purpose. That means God wrote that purpose for your life from eternity, and nothing and no one can change it. Make sure, brother and sister, that you have a divine purpose, because if you have no purpose to live for, then you qualify to die. But as long as you have a purpose, you will not leave this world until you fulfill it.

What is God's idea in allowing these trials and struggles to shape you? Are you listening? He shapes you so you will look like Christ, have Christ's character, and have conviction. We have a Christianity based on emotion, but not conviction. Emotions pass, and people who live by emotions live according to their circumstances. "It's very cold today, so I will not go to church." Now it is too hot. "That person even greeted me." They live by greetings: today I am happy, tomorrow I am sad. I do not live by emotions or the circumstances around me. I live by a revelation I have received. Someone praise His name.

You have to understand something, brother. Are you listening? This is not about what surrounds you. If you focus on what surrounds you, you will hear, "Look at the situation," and even, "Look at what is happening with immigration." I do not live by what surrounds me. I live by what is inside me, because what surrounds us is temporary, but what is inside me is eternal. That is why John says, "Greater is He who is in us than he who is in the world." Someone say, "Glory to God."

Would you like me to tell you a secret? I have learned to seek God not outside of me, but within me. Look, God does not want to visit us; God wants to live in us. What you have inside is indestructible, but we do not show it. Let just a little of what you have inside come out. That is all you can do? Someone praise His name. When the enemy sees you, he does not see you; he sees that Christ defeated him on Calvary's cross. Glory to God.

God Has Already Done It All

How could the Lord leave us at this level when God has already done everything for us? Tell the person who is a little quiet, "God has already done it all." I do not have to add anything or take anything away. He has already done it all. Many preachers and evangelists make this mistake, but God has already done it all. He gave His only Son and loved the world. Listen to what I discovered: God does not have a problem with the world, because He loved it. The world is the one that has a problem with God. That is why preachers are reconciling the world to God, not God to the world. Sometimes we present God as if He wanted to kill everyone. He still loves the world. The world is the one that has a problem with God, and we are reconciling the world to Him. Do you understand? He has already done it all for you. I do not have to add anything to or take anything away from the plan of salvation. It is already done.

Look at how the apostle Paul presents it, because today we are going to study apostolic doctrine:

"For whom He foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, that He might be the firstborn among many brethren."

Brothers and sisters, are you ready for what I am about to say? Christ died on the cross as the only begotten Son, but He rose again as the firstborn. Firstborn. You said it. Someone say, "I will say it again." God has only one unique, only-begotten Son. Jesus died on the cross as the only Son of God, but when He rose, He was the firstborn from the dead. I am not God's only-begotten Son, but I am a firstborn. Hallelujah. And firstborn children receive a double inheritance. I am not preaching to slaves; I am preaching to firstborn children who have an eternal inheritance the enemy cannot take away. Tell the person who is a little quiet, "Act like a firstborn."

We will pause here, because I want to take you through seven questions the apostle Paul asks in Romans, and we will answer them one by one.

"Moreover whom He predestined, these He also called; whom He called, these He also justified; and whom He justified, these He also glorified."

He called me, predestined me, justified me, and also glorified me. Did the Lord know...? Why don't we praise the Lord as if today were our last day on earth? Better yet, let us praise Him as if today were our first day in heaven. Brother, I do not see myself in hell; I see myself with Christ. Someone say, "He did it all, He did it all."

First Question

Then Paul asks the first question. You may sit down. The first question comes in Romans 8:31: "What then shall we say to these things?" Paul is asking, "What more can we say?" That is why He said on the cross, "It is finished." It is already done. I feel complete; I am a child of God. I am a child of God now, and I will be like Him. It is already done, because that is how He sees it.

Second Question

Then the apostle Paul introduces the second question. Brother, Paul seems to puff out his chest and say:

"If God is for us, who can be against us?"

I think you are starting to understand the confidence. If God is for you, who can be against you? If God is for me, who can be against me? The enemy has to be quiet, because to destroy you he would have to destroy God, and that is impossible. Is there someone here who can praise God and say, "He has been for me in trials, in struggles, in criticism, in sickness, in an accident, and at work"? We can shout, "God is for me, God is for me, God is for me." But say it believing.

Third Question

Brother, Paul introduces the third question. I like this one:

"He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, how shall He not with Him also freely give us all things?"

I do not know that word, "spare." The One who gave us His Son, His only Son, gave Him to us as a gift. So the question is: how will He not give us all things with Him? Tell the person next to you, "Do not settle for just anything when God wants to give us all things."

I said, "Lord, the gift You gave me, Your only Son, made me feel very special." When you give someone a gift, it is because that person is special to you, right? And the more expensive the gift, the more special you are to me. I do not know how you feel about this gift, but I feel very special, because God gave me His only Son, the most expensive gift in the universe. I thought you were listening. The reason many people do not enjoy the Christian life is that they do not enjoy the gift.

When my wife gives me a birthday present, I get serious, take the gift with a smile, and want to unwrap what is inside. "Thank you." God's love was not shown through words, but through actions: He gave us a gift. Someone say, "Glory to God." I would have been content if the Lord had given me a galaxy, millions and millions of stars. "Give me a planet, streets of gold." But God gave something more precious, the most expensive gift in the universe. Someone praise His name. I love this gift: it cost God dearly, but it is free to you. The gift is free to you, but very costly to God. Let us not make the church cheap. Do not give the Lord cheap praise when you know the price paid for your soul. Give Him something.

This gift is special because you can give someone a gift that means nothing, but this gift matters. Do you know why? Because God Himself is in the gift. The gift is not separate from the giver: the Father is in the Son. That is why John said, "He who has the Son has the Father." Someone say, "God is the gift." Brother, I am holding myself back. I feel rivers of living water. That is why the apostle Paul says that all the fullness of the Godhead dwells in the gift. Hallelujah.

Let us keep going. Look at what I am going to tell you, to make the enemy grind his teeth and get even angrier: God allowed the enemy to unwrap this gift at Calvary. He thought he would kill the gift for humanity, for this church, but at Calvary, on the cross, the gift was opened for us. They placed a crown of thorns on Him and pierced Him with a spear and nails. But what came from that gift? The precious blood of Jesus. Glory to God.

Do you know why many people leave God's path? Do you know why people do not praise God? Because they still do not know the price paid for their soul. But how many here know that eternal life came from that gift? It is an eternal gift. I will give a minute to those who are enjoying Christ. The enemy cannot take this gift away.

Brother, I do not want to see the New Jerusalem first. Lord, I do not want to go to the New Jerusalem if Jesus is not there. I want to see the Lord, the One who paid the price. When He rose again, He did not erase the wounds from His body. Imagine this: millions and millions of angels praise Jesus today, and when they see those wounds, they ask the Lord, "Who were those wounds for?" Who do you think the Lord would point to? The people here. Each one of us. A wound is not pleasant to see; I have seen many wounds. But Christ's wounds produced beauty, because sin made you ugly, but through those wounds we have been transformed. We are the ones who should praise God the most right now. Why are some of you so quiet?

Fourth Question

How many questions have we had? Someone is listening. The apostle Paul asks the fourth question, saying: if Christ has done it all for me...

"Who shall bring a charge against God's elect? It is God who justifies."

When I studied this, I pictured a courtroom where God is the judge, the enemy is the accuser, and Christ is our lawyer. You enter that courtroom as the accused. The enemy begins to accuse you, not about the future ahead of you, but about your past. Satan always goes back to your past, because he has nothing to accuse you of in your future. How many brothers and sisters suffer because of the past, because of what was done to them, even in childhood, and still carry it? Today I want to tell you that we will be set free from the past. Why? Because Christ erased your past with His blood. What matters to the church now is your present and your future. Who drives backward? We always move forward. How many are moving forward? Tell the person who is quiet, "The past is a little way behind us." The farther you move toward heaven, the less you will see the past and the more you will see the Lord.

Who will accuse us? Say, "No one." The Bible says Satan accuses us day and night, brothers and sisters. He brings up our past or any mistake we made in the present. "Look, Lord, that church does not deserve to go to heaven. Look at their past, look at what they did yesterday." He brings out the darkness of our past, and Jesus brings out His blood. I will say something that should make you praise God: when the darkness of my sins mixes with the blood of Jesus Christ, it comes out white. Help me praise the Lord!

Fifth Question

When Paul removes the accusation, the fifth question removes condemnation:

"Who is he who condemns? It is Christ who died, and furthermore is also risen, who is even at the right hand of God, who also makes intercession for us."

Brother, do not act like someone condemned; act like someone redeemed. I went to a courtroom. Have you ever been to a courtroom, where the judge and jury sit, and the condemned person is waiting for a life sentence? Some people have fainted. Suddenly the judge tells the condemned person, "The jury has found you innocent." How did that person leave the courtroom, when they were on their way to prison and now they are going home? Do you think that is how I feel? Do you know how I feel, when I was on my way to hell but the blood of Christ...? Someone say, "Glory to God." I can declare that we are not on our way to hell, but on our way to heaven.

I will say this and see if we praise the Lord even more: if people on their way to hell shout, if a drunk person shouts in a bar, how much more should we, who are on our way to heaven! Blessed be His name. Tell the person next to you, "There is no condemnation; there is no condemnation." Prisons are for the guilty. It is unjust to put an innocent person in prison. If you are innocent and justified, it would be unjust even for God to send you to hell. It will be justice for you to enter heaven. If you do not know where you are going, then stay quiet. Do you know where you are going? There is no accusation and no condemnation. To the nervous brother next to you: relax. Do you know who is the only one who could condemn us? Christ. But the Lord would say, "How could I condemn them? I died, was buried, rose again, am in heaven, and intercede for them there." Glory to God.

Sixth Question

Then Paul asks the sixth question, brothers and sisters, and I like this one so much. Are you ready? Are you ready? Say it with me:

"Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?"

I will say it again. Do not quiet down your praise. Who can separate us from the love of Christ? Not the love you have for Christ, but the love Christ has for us. Paul mentions seven enemies: tribulation, distress, persecution, famine, nakedness, peril, and sword. Look at the confidence I have in Christ, because I know He loves me with an eternal love. Do you know what I have understood? Listen: all your enemies are passing and temporary, but the love of Christ is not. So I can say: death, you are temporary; sickness, you are temporary; world, you are temporary; demons, you are temporary—all the enemies of the church are temporary. But the love I have is not temporary. Someone praise His name. I do not praise something temporary, brother; I am here for something eternal. Help me praise the Lord.

Seventh Question: More Than Conquerors

And when Paul answers those questions, brother, he says:

"Yet in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him who loved us."

Say it correctly. Look at what it says: it does not say we are conquerors, but more than conquerors. I want to know how many of you are more than conquerors. It says we are more than champions. How do champions act? I will ask again: how does a champion act? How does someone who is more than a conqueror act? Tell the person who is quiet, "Brother, I am not defeated today. This church is not defeated. Today this church is celebrating victory in Christ." Who wants to celebrate victory in Christ? This is tremendous. Keep praising the Lord. There is a victor here, a victorious woman here, and another victor here. Hallelujah.

I have understood something. The Bible teaches me that Christ is the Victor, but the church is more. How can I explain it? A boxer earns 20 million dollars by defeating his opponent. He gets home and his wife congratulates him. But who did it? Christ is the Victor, and He tells the church, "Celebrate this victory." I am not preaching to a defeated church or an orphaned church. I am preaching to a church that is more, more, more. Shake the person who is quiet and tell them, "More, more, more." Someone say, "Glory to God." The enemy is defeated, death is defeated, and the church is victorious. I will give a minute to those who have victory: shout, "Victory!"

Look, brother, I am almost done. Those who are standing, stay standing. First Paul mentions visible enemies, like tribulation and the sword. Now he mentions invisible enemies, because the church has visible and invisible enemies. I would rather face an enemy I can see; for an invisible one, we need the Holy Spirit. And Paul says this—are you ready?

"For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities nor powers, nor things present nor things to come, nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord."

He names ten enemies. Blessed be His name. Hallelujah, hallelujah. Raise your hand like a victor and say this morning, "I have something greater than death. I have something greater than this world. I have something greater than angels, principalities, powers, things present, and things to come." Hallelujah. This eternal love will one day take me out of this world and bring me to the God who loved me. Hallelujah, hallelujah.

The enemy wants to see your hand down and your mouth closed, as if you were worth nothing and we were defeated. That is why we do the opposite. I do not shout to get the victory; I shout because I already have the victory. I can praise God. I do not know how many victors are here this morning, but let someone who is victorious say, "God is for me, God is for me." Hallelujah. Tell the person who is quiet, "Christ died for me; He shed His blood for me."

Keep praising the Lord. Say with me, "Because of the love God showed on Calvary's cross, the door of hell was closed to me and the door of heaven was opened." Victors, raise your hands. Do not be a spectator; be a worshiper.

Altar Call

As I said at the beginning, I want to invite to the altar the brothers and sisters who have gone through trials and struggles and thought they would not make it. I want to stand with brothers and sisters of conviction. People who say, "If He died for me, I will also die for Him."

I do not know if I have told you this. I want you to come to this altar. As a testimony: about 16 years ago, my brother, who lives in California, called me at six on a Sunday evening after church. I thought he was calling to say hello, but when I answered, he was screaming, literally shouting. He said, "My 15-year-old daughter just had an accident leaving church." They pronounced her dead because it was an instant death, as she was leaving the service where the Lord had been praised. His daughter was lying there after an accident.

I want to speak to those who have faced trials and struggles and will not deny the Lord. My brother and sister-in-law told me that at three in the morning, two demons visited them: sadness and depression. They heard the enemy's voice saying, "How nice—you praised God this afternoon. Congratulations. But dare to praise your Lord now, when your daughter is in the morgue. What would you do, brother and sister?" But this Apostolic Church has martyrs who did not deny their faith even among lions or in the flames. Would you deny the Lord over anything?

My brother, sister-in-law, and brother told me, "The enemy thought he would silence us, but we praised the Lord even more, because our daughter is not in the morgue; her soul is in the Lord's presence." How many brothers and sisters of conviction are here? I want to invite to the altar someone the enemy has not been able to silence. You still want to serve God; you still want to preach this gospel to every creature. Come to the altar as a victor.

Pastor, I want to confess something to you before God: I have already made an agreement with the Lord. If I have to leave this world as a martyr, it will be a great honor for me. I have missionary friends who have gone to Muslim countries. Do you know what they said? "We already brought our coffin." I am speaking to brothers and sisters with conviction, who will not deny the faith, even if persecution comes. We have to make our commitment to the Lord now. If they tell us, "Deny the Lord," I will not deny Him. If I try to save my life, I will lose it; but if I lose it for Christ, I will gain it. Praise God in victory. Shout for victory. Louder, louder, louder! God did not call you here to be defeated, but to be victorious.

Conviction. The word "conviction" comes from "convict": someone who has been convicted. The judge says, "You have been convicted for life." Paul called himself a prisoner of Christ for the sake of the gospel. Hallelujah, hallelujah.

I had to preach at that funeral, Pastor, and that night, during the service, 100 souls came forward to give their lives to the Lord. I called them, and they stood behind me, and we declared that girl was a seed of revival. Hallelujah, hallelujah.

Let us worship God as victors, as people who have victory. Do you know what I learned? I learned to complain less and praise more. Do not complain so much, brother or sister. Do not complain so much. Take your time, brother; we are not in a hurry. When you get up, get up like a victor. "I will serve God, I will serve God, no matter what comes."

Lord, may there be a brother or sister who says with conviction, "I will not deny my faith. I will not deny You. I will confess You before people."
